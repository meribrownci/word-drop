Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

Word Drop: Falling Letters respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you play the game.

Information We Collect

Word Drop uses Firebase services provided by Google to support its global leaderboard.

These services may process:

  • An automatically generated anonymous user ID
  • Game scores and leaderboard information
  • Technical information processed by Firebase to operate and secure its services

How We Use Information

We use this information to operate the game, save and display leaderboard scores, and maintain the reliability and security of the service.

Third-Party Services

Word Drop uses Google Firebase, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Information processed by Firebase is subject to Google's applicable privacy practices.

Learn more: Firebase Privacy and Security

Advertising and Tracking

Word Drop does not use leaderboard information to track players across other companies' apps or websites for advertising.

Data Retention and Deletion

Leaderboard information may be retained while the leaderboard service operates. To request deletion of information associated with your game, contact us using the email below. We may need information to locate the relevant leaderboard record.

Children's Privacy

Word Drop does not require players to provide their name or email address to play. If you are a parent or guardian with a privacy concern, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the game evolves. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
